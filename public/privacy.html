Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

QuickShare Pro was engineered with privacy as a foundational constraint, not an afterthought. We do not require accounts, we do not log personal identities, and we do not monetize user data.

1. Ephemeral Data Lifecycle

All shared content (photos, videos, documents, clipboard items, and messages) resides in temporary, isolated rooms. Rooms automatically expire and permanently delete all contents after their configured time-to-live (1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days).

When an end-session event occurs or the timer expires, room records and uploaded files on disk or cloud storage are immediately unlinked and deleted.

2. No Analytics or Third-Party Trackers

QuickShare Pro contains zero tracking cookies, advertising scripts, or third-party behavioral analytics. Fonts are self-hosted directly from our servers to prevent external telemetry leakage.

3. Security Controls

  • Session tokens are HMAC-signed and strictly bound to individual room IDs.
  • Rate-limiting is enforced to prevent automated enumeration or brute-force attacks.
  • Strict Content Security Policies (CSP) and forced download headers prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).

4. Contact & Questions

For questions regarding our privacy architecture, please contact us via the abuse and reporting portal.
